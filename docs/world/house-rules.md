# House Rules

The rules this table plays by, where they differ from the books. Keep this page short — it's the one players will actually read.

## Healing

- **Potions** are a bonus action to drink yourself, an action to give to someone else.
- **Short rests** take ten minutes, not an hour.

## Inspiration

The DM hands out inspiration for great roleplay, a clever plan, or making the table laugh. You can hold one at a time, and give it to another player.

## Critical hits

Roll the damage dice twice; on a natural 20, also add one more die of the weapon's damage.
