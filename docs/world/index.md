# The Lantern Coast

*A grey coast of marsh, fog, and fishing towns, where every harbor keeps a lantern lit at night.*

The Lantern Coast runs north from the river mouth to the cliffs of the Hollow Head: a hundred miles of reed-marsh, shingle, and small towns that live on the sea and fear it in equal measure. The roads are bad and the fog is worse; most travel is by boat.

## What everyone knows

- **The lanterns.** Every harbor on the coast keeps a lantern burning through the night. Ask why and you'll get a different answer in each town — for the fleet, for luck, "for the drowned."
- **The tides.** Spring tides come twice a month and flood the marsh roads. Nobody sensible travels on a spring-tide night.
- **Greywater** is the biggest town on the coast, which isn't saying much.
