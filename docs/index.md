## Now

### Where we are

The party is in **Greywater**, level 3, two nights after arriving on the Lantern Coast. They've been asked by [[Sister Amara Wells]] to find the chapel's missing bell, and [[Pip Larkin]] has just sold them the best lead they've had: a boat rowing out to the [[Bellwether Light]] the night the bell vanished.

### Open threads

- **The missing bell** — taken from the [[Chapel of the Tides]] on the night of the spring tide.
- **The Tidecallers** — a name people whisper around the harbor, and won't say twice.
- **Kestrel's debt** — twenty gold owed to [[Pip Larkin]], who mentioned it again.

??? dm "DM only — what's really going on"
    [[Magistrate Corvin Sloane]] funds the [[The Tidecallers|Tidecallers]]. The next spring tide is in four days; if the bell is rung then, everyone in Greywater who has ever drowned and been brought back walks into the sea - starting with Sloane himself, who wants exactly that.

## Opening — previously

!!! read-aloud "Previously…"
    You came to Greywater for work and found a town holding its breath. The bell of the Chapel of the Tides is gone — stolen on the night of the spring tide — and Sister Amara has asked you to bring it back before the fleet's luck runs out. Last night, over eel pie at the Gull & Lantern, a halfling named Pip Larkin leaned across the table and told you what he saw: a boat with no lantern, rowing out toward the old drowned lighthouse in the marsh.

    This morning, the fog is thick on the harbor, and somebody has chalked a wave on the inn's door.

## Asking around the quay

Most of Greywater wants the bell back and won't say a word about the Tidecallers. Each conversation is worth one thing:

- **[[Hester Vane]]** — the chalked wave is a warning mark; her grandmother saw them on doors "the last time." She'll point them to the inn's cellar if they ask about the lighthouse.
- **[[Captain Oren Hask]]** — the Watch "looked into it." He'd love help and can't admit it. Persuasion DC 12 gets him to say the Magistrate told him to drop it.
- **The fishmarket** — Insight DC 13 notices which stallholders went pale at the word *Tidecallers*. One of them follows the party at dusk.

!!! dm "Tactics for the follower"
    The follower is a [[Tidecaller]] scout. If spotted, it runs for the water and swims under the quay. If not, it signals the ambush.

## Ambush on the quay

At dusk, as the party crosses the quay, four Tidecallers in oilskin coats step out from between the fish-sheds, and something large moves under the water at the quay's edge.

{{ encounter dock-ambush }}

!!! read-aloud "Dusk on the quay"
    The fish-sheds are shut and the gulls have gone quiet. Four figures in long oilskin coats step out of the fog, hooks in their hands, and behind you the water at the quay's edge begins to bubble.

- **Terrain:** the quay is 20 feet wide, with the harbor on one side. Anyone pulled into the water by a gaff hook is in the [[Bog Lurker]]'s reach.
- **Morale:** the Tidecallers flee into the water when two of them fall. One taken alive knows the rite is "when the tide comes back."
