# Greywater — Events & Hooks

Drop-in scenes for when the table wanders off the plan. Each stands on its own; use what fits. (This page says `dm_only: true` at the top, so it never appears on the players' site.)

## The Eel Pie Contest

!!! read-aloud "A chalkboard outside the Gull"
    EEL PIE CONTEST — FRIDAY. Winner eats free for a month. Loser does the dishes. (Hester's rules: no magic, no Pip.)

[[Hester Vane]] judges. Anyone who enters gets a Constitution save (DC 12) per slice; whoever eats the most wins a month of free meals — and the fishermen at the next table, impressed, start talking about what they've seen out on the marsh at night.

## The Watch Needs Hands

[[Captain Oren Hask]] is two watchmen short after a brawl. He'll pay 5 sp a night to anyone who'll walk the quay with a lantern. A quiet night — until the fog comes in and someone starts ringing a handbell out on the water.

## Something in the Nets

A fishing boat comes in with something tangled in its nets: a drowned man in an oilskin coat, three days dead, with a wave tattooed on his wrist. The fishermen want it off their boat. [[Sister Amara Wells]] wants it buried properly. The Watch wants it quiet.
