# Greywater

*Fog, fish, and a green lantern that never goes out.*

Greywater is a fishing town of about two thousand people, built where the marsh meets the Grey Sound. The working town crowds the quay; the people who own the boats live up the hill in Upper Town, where the streets stay dry at spring tide.

{{ map greywater }}

## Districts

- **The Quay** — the harbor wall, the fishmarket, the Gull & Lantern, and the Watch House.
- **Upper Town** — the Chapel of the Tides, the Magistrate's Hall, and the big slate-roofed houses.
- **The Marsh Road** — a causeway north through the reeds to the old drowned lighthouse.

## Who holds power

[[Magistrate Corvin Sloane]] rules the town in all but name: he holds the courts, the records, and the debts on half the boats in the harbor. [[Captain Oren Hask]] and his Watch keep the peace, as far as six people can.
