# At the table

## Before the session

Put everything for the next session on **[This Session](../index.md)** — it's the home page, so it's the first thing you see:

1. **Now** at the top: where the party is, the open threads, and a DM box of what's really going on.
2. **A note box** for the session: the arc, the starting point, the running order, and drop-ins if the table wanders.
3. **The opening:** a "Previously…" recap to read aloud.
4. **The scenes**, in order, with their read-alouds, encounters, and stat blocks.

Prep for later sessions stays on its arc page.

## During the session

- **Run fights** from [Encounters](../encounters/index.md): pick an encounter, **Run** it, and the initiative tracker takes over — initiative, hit points, conditions, turns, and the character cards beside the list. With the players' site online, **Share with players** shows the order on their phones.
- **Hover names** for quick cards; click for the full entry. Hover a condition in the initiative tracker for what it does.
- **Look up a rule** on the [Rules](../rules/index.md) tab, or keep the [DM Screen](../rules/dm-screen.md) open in a second tab.
- **Give out loot** on the [Items](../reference/items.md) page: the **Held by** picker records who carries what (with `CampaignCodex5e` running).
- **Record** the session, if your table agrees — see [Recording sessions](recording.md).

`CampaignCodex5e` must be running for anything that saves (the builder, the editors, the recorder). Without it, the site still reads fine — even the copy `codex build` makes, opened straight from disk.

## After the session

1. **Write the session's page**: a new file in `docs/sessions/` — copy the last one, and give it the next `order:` number. It appears in the Sessions tab by itself, newest first. On it: a "Previously…" recap for next time, what happened in order, a few lines from the table, and a DM box for what the players don't know. Everyone and everything you `[[mention]]` in the public part appears on the players' site.
2. **Move the played prep** from This Session back into its arc page, keeping the wording — it's the record of what you planned.
3. **Update Now**, and start the next session's prep.
4. **Update the data:** someone died (`status: dead`), moved, joined a faction; an item changed hands.
5. **Back up** (`codex backup`, or commit), and **publish** if you're online.
