# Rules

The game's rules, ready to search mid-session: the **5e System Reference Document 5.1**. Use the search box at the top of any page, or start here:

- **[DM Screen](dm-screen.md)** — the tables you reach for during play, on one page.
- **[Conditions](conditions.md)** — blinded to unconscious.
- **[Combat](combat.md)** — the order of combat, actions, attacking, cover, damage and healing, mounted and underwater combat.
- **[Abilities & checks](abilities.md)** — ability checks, skills, saving throws, inspiration.
- **[Adventuring](adventuring.md)** — time, movement and travel, the environment, resting, objects, traps, poisons, diseases, madness, and life between adventures.
- **[Spellcasting](spellcasting.md)** — how magic works.
- **[Spells](spells.md)** — every spell, filterable by level, class, and school.
- **[Magic Items](magic-items.md)** — attunement and the rest of the rules, then every item.
- **[Equipment](equipment.md)** — coins, armor, weapons, gear, tools, mounts, and costs.
- **[Characters](characters.md)** — races, backgrounds, alignment, languages, feats, leveling up, multiclassing.
- **[Monsters, NPCs & the planes](more.md)** — reading stat blocks, NPCs, the planes, and pantheons.

**Hover cards everywhere.** Write `[[Prone]]`, `[[Fireball]]`, or `[[Bag of Holding]]` on any page for a link with the full text on hover — and condition words in spells and rules (*knocked prone*, *is restrained*) are linked for you. In the initiative tracker, hover a condition to see what it does. Your own campaign's people, places, and items always come first if a name matches.
