## On your turn

**Move** up to your speed, and take **one action**, plus a **bonus action** if something gives you one. Interact with one object for free (draw a sword, open a door). **One reaction** per round, on your turn or anyone's.

| Action | Does |
|---|---|
| Attack | one weapon attack (more with Extra Attack) |
| Cast a Spell | a spell with a casting time of 1 action |
| Dash | gain extra movement equal to your speed |
| Disengage | your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks this turn |
| Dodge | attacks against you have disadvantage; Dex saves with advantage |
| Help | an ally gets advantage on their next check, or their next attack against a creature within 5 feet of you |
| Hide | a Dexterity (Stealth) check |
| Ready | choose a trigger and an action; react when it happens (readying a spell takes concentration) |
| Search | a Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check |
| Use an Object | anything beyond your one free object interaction |

**Opportunity attack:** when a hostile creature you can see leaves your reach, you can use your reaction to make one melee attack against it.

**Grapple / shove:** replace one attack with a Strength (Athletics) check against the target's Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics); the target can be no more than one size larger than you. A shove knocks the target prone or pushes it 5 feet.

More: [Combat](combat.md).

## Exhaustion

| Level | Effect (cumulative) |
|:-:|---|
| 1 | Disadvantage on ability checks |
| 2 | Speed halved |
| 3 | Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws |
| 4 | Hit point maximum halved |
| 5 | Speed reduced to 0 |
| 6 | Death |

A long rest, with food and drink, removes one level.

## Dying

- At 0 hit points you fall [[Unconscious]] and make a **death saving throw** at the start of each turn: 10 or higher succeeds.
- **Three successes:** stable. **Three failures:** dead. A **natural 20** regains 1 hit point; a **natural 1** counts as two failures.
- **Damage at 0 hit points** is a failure (a critical hit, two). **Instant death** if the damage left over after dropping to 0 equals or exceeds your hit point maximum.
- **Stabilize** someone with a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check.

**Concentration:** when you take damage while concentrating, make a Constitution save — DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher. Being incapacitated or killed ends it.

## Resting

| | Takes | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Short rest | 1 hour | spend Hit Dice to heal (each die + Con modifier) |
| Long rest | 8 hours (no more than 2 hours of watch or light activity) | all hit points; back half your Hit Dice (at least 1); one level of exhaustion removed |

Only one long rest in 24 hours, and you need at least 1 hit point to start one. More: [Adventuring](adventuring.md#rule-rest).

## Travel

| Pace | Per minute | Per hour | Per day | Effect |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|---|
| Fast | 400 ft | 4 miles | 30 miles | −5 to passive Wisdom (Perception) |
| Normal | 300 ft | 3 miles | 24 miles | — |
| Slow | 200 ft | 2 miles | 18 miles | can use stealth |

**Forced march:** past 8 hours, a Constitution save each extra hour (DC 10 + 1 per extra hour) or gain a level of exhaustion. **Difficult terrain:** every foot costs 2.

## Light and vision

- **Lightly obscured** (dim light, patchy fog): disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
- **Heavily obscured** (darkness, thick fog): effectively [[Blinded]].
- **Darkvision:** dim light counts as bright, and darkness as dim, within its range — in shades of gray.

## Hazards

- **Falling:** 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet fallen (maximum 20d6), and you land [[Prone]].
- **Suffocating:** hold your breath for 1 + Constitution modifier minutes (at least 30 seconds). Then you last Constitution modifier rounds (at least 1) before dropping to 0 hit points.
